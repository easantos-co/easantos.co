About the Firm

Built on execution, not credentials.

E A Santos Investments Inc. was founded by Eli Santos on a straightforward premise: the discipline built on a job site carries directly into the discipline required to manage capital. Eli's path began on the tools as a Welder by Trade, later serving as a site manager overseeing complex construction projects across Metro Vancouver, before he turned that same discipline toward real estate.

Over a decade, he grew a portfolio from four properties to just over two hundred units, partnering directly with investors at every stage. That portfolio was exited through a structured sale, and the proceeds and the relationships behind them now form the foundation of this firm.

E A Santos Investments Inc. is the next chapter — a disciplined, partnership-led vehicle for capital that expects the same hands-on standard Eli has applied since day one. That standard is now extending beyond Metro Vancouver: the firm's footprint has grown to include commercial properties in Florida and residential units in San Diego, California, as this next chapter shifts toward opportunities across the U.S.

Our Approach

Three principles guide every position we take.

Discipline

Underwrite like an owner.

Every opportunity is assessed the way Eli assessed his own properties — against real cash flow, real construction cost, and real downside, not projections alone.

Partnership

Align with our investors.

Our growth has always come through direct partnership rather than passive capital. Investors are brought in as partners in the outcome, not just contributors to it.

Execution

Do the work ourselves.

From site walks to structuring the exit, Eli's background is in doing the work directly. That same hands-on standard governs how this firm sources and manages every position.
